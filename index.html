Everything started in 1998 with my first CRT computer. Tearing open hardware, figuring out how systems communicate, and building from scratch became my baseline way of seeing the world.

Taking computers apart in my childhood taught me never to fear complexity. Today, I use automation and artificial intelligence for what truly matters: stripping away bureaucracy and making technology work for us.

Philosophy teaches me to question assumptions before designing solutions, and music reminds me every day that technical precision only matters when all the parts operate in harmony.

In my corporate life, I serve as a Customer Success Manager for Global B2B tech accounts. On the other half of life, music ties it all together: over 20 years on strings, drums, and composition.

// ROOTS & COMPOSITION

I studied Music Composition and Conducting at the School of Music and Fine Arts, alongside my wife and partner. Music is a core pillar linked to our everyday lives.
